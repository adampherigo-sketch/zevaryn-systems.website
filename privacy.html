SITE POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

Introduction

Zevaryn Systems respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit zevarynsystems.com, submit a project inquiry, or contact Zevaryn Systems, and how that information may be used.

Information You Provide

You may provide information to Zevaryn Systems when submitting a project inquiry or contacting the business directly.

Depending on how you contact us, this information may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business or organization name
  • Current website address
  • Type of project or service requested
  • Budget range
  • Project timeline
  • Project details or other information you choose to provide
  • How you heard about Zevaryn Systems

The project inquiry form also includes a required acknowledgment confirming that submitting an inquiry does not create a binding agreement.

How Information Is Used

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Discuss a potential project
  • Evaluate your requested services and project requirements
  • Communicate with you about your request
  • Maintain appropriate business and communication records
  • Detect or reduce spam, abuse, or misuse of the website

Project inquiry information is not currently used for unrelated marketing communications unless you separately choose to receive them in the future.

Zevaryn Systems does not sell personal information provided through project inquiries.

Project Inquiry Processing

The Start a Project form is processed using Netlify Forms.

When you submit the form, the information entered into it may be transmitted to and processed through Netlify as part of providing the form-submission service.

Netlify's own terms and privacy practices may apply to its processing of that information.

Zevaryn Systems does not intentionally store project inquiry information in your browser using localStorage or sessionStorage.

Session Storage

This website uses browser sessionStorage for a limited interface function.

A session value named "zevarynEntered" may be stored to remember that you have entered the website during the current browser session.

This information is used only for site interface behavior.

It is not used for advertising, analytics, behavioral profiling, or cross-site tracking.

Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

The current Zevaryn Systems website does not intentionally use:

  • Advertising cookies
  • Advertising pixels
  • Google Analytics
  • Meta Pixel
  • Behavioral advertising systems
  • Cross-site tracking systems
  • User fingerprinting

If these practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy should be updated accordingly.

Google Fonts

This website uses Google Fonts to provide certain typefaces used in the site's design.

Your browser may connect to Google-hosted resources when loading these fonts. Google's own terms and privacy practices may apply to those requests.

Data Retention

Information from project inquiries and related communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry, maintain appropriate business records, manage related communications, resolve disputes, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Zevaryn Systems does not currently publish a fixed retention period for project inquiries.

Data Security

Reasonable care is taken when handling information submitted through the website.

However, no internet transmission, hosting platform, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Requests and Choices

You may contact Zevaryn Systems to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that you previously provided.

Zevaryn Systems will respond to appropriate requests as reasonably possible and as required by applicable law.

External Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites, including websites displayed as part of the Zevaryn Systems portfolio.

Zevaryn Systems is not responsible for the content, availability, security, or privacy practices of third-party websites.

Visitors should review the privacy practices of those websites separately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Zevaryn Systems, this website, or the services used by the website change.

When material updates are made, the effective or last-updated date on this page may be revised.
